Privacy Policy for 9 Ball League Practice Last updated: September 9, 2026 9 Ball League Practice is a scorekeeping and practice application designed for 9-ball pool players. INFORMATION WE COLLECT 9 Ball League Practice does not require users to create an account. The app does not intentionally collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information with the developer or third parties. LOCALLY STORED DATA Information entered into the app, including player names, scores, targets, match information, and practice history, is stored locally on the user's device for the purpose of providing the app's scorekeeping features. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES The app does not currently use advertising services, analytics services, or third-party tracking services. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children. DATA SECURITY Because app data is stored locally on the user's device, users are responsible for maintaining the security of their device. CHANGES TO THIS PRIVACY POLICY This Privacy Policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change. Updates will be posted on this page. DISCLAIMER 9 Ball League Practice is an independent practice and scorekeeping application and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the American Poolplayers Association (APA). CONTACT Questions regarding this Privacy Policy may be directed to the developer through the contact information provided on the app's Google Play listing.
